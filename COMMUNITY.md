# 💚 Community Credits

Wilds of Hoenn grows with its players. A big thank you to everyone in the Discord community
who plays the mod, reports bugs, shares screenshots and suggests ideas: you shape what this
mod becomes. Every idea below came from a player and made it into the game.

Have an idea of your own? Share it on Discord or open an issue here, and you may end up on
this list.

## 🌱 Ideas and Suggestions

| Idea | Suggested by |
| --- | --- |
| **Sweet Scent in the overworld:** Sweet Scent calls wild Pokémon out to you, waiting with hearts over their heads. | krustykong |
| **Retrieving followers while biking:** your partner rests in its Poké Ball while you ride. | krustykong |
| **Retrieving followers in Pokémon Centers and cutscenes:** your partner steps aside into its ball while your team is healed and during story scenes. | benjamindatum |
| **Follower affection reactions:** your partner reacts to being petted, played with and talked to, showing how it feels. | getuseful |
| **Overworld running reactions:** wild Pokémon notice you running past, the weak ones fleeing and the strong ones giving chase. | getuseful |
| **Marine life ambience:** fish leap, whales surface and spout, and sea creatures swim by beneath the waves. | tedwards75 (GitHub) |

---

Thank you for playing, testing and dreaming up new things with us. 🌿
